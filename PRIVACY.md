# Privacy Policy — fusion360-isogrid

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

**We don't collect your data, and we don't want it.**

fusion360-isogrid runs locally inside Autodesk Fusion. We don't collect your models, geometry, settings, usage statistics, personal information, or telemetry. We don't operate a data-collection server, require an account, or sell or share user data.

The add-in processes your design locally. If you save parameter presets, they are stored in a JSON file on your computer.

**Network activity:** The GitHub-distributed version checks GitHub for available updates when you open the command and downloads an update only when you choose to install it. These requests go to GitHub, whose own privacy policy applies. **The Autodesk Marketplace version will omit the built-in updater and its network requests.**

Autodesk Fusion and Autodesk Marketplace operate under Autodesk's own privacy terms. If you voluntarily open a support issue on GitHub, GitHub's privacy settings apply to what you choose to post.

Questions: [fusion360-isogrid GitHub issues](https://github.com/zyeborm/fusion360-isogrid/issues).
